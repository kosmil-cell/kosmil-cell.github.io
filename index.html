Build better Pokémon teams.

Team Factory is a Pokédex, roster manager and smart team builder for casual and competitive players.

Free on Android, with an optional Premium upgrade.

Everything in the app

From browsing the Pokédex to rating a finished team. Some features are part of Premium and are marked below.

  • Pokédex

    Browse every Pokémon and filter by type, competitive tier and best switch-in partners.

  • Roster builder

    Add Pokémon from the Pokédex, scan screenshots from your game or import from Pokémon Showdown. Roles are assigned for you.

  • Smart team building

    Tags show which roles your team is missing and suggest Pokémon that fill them.

  • Team rating Premium

    Score your team from 0 to 100 for casual or competitive play, with an explanation for every point.

  • Move optimizer Premium

    Find the move combination that gives your Pokémon the widest offensive coverage.

  • Win-rate analytics

    See which partner pairings win most often, based on millions of battles.

  • EV and encounter guide

    Find training spots and encounter data for every game.

  • Fun modes and quizzes

    Draft from random pools in Team Factory mode, build within a set cost in Budget mode (both Premium), or test your knowledge with quizzes.
